IMPLEMENTED

- Each chain owns independent routing, race, hedge, timeout, retry and batching
  settings in src/config/rpc-policies.ts and its own learned transport state.
- Removed HTTP batching delays on the six chains where batching was enabled.
  Application Multicall3 batching continues to group contract reads.
- Added a delayed backup on multi-provider chains. A deterministic two-provider
  stall test improved from approximately 10.1 seconds to 351-353 milliseconds.
- Persisted a Bun patch for smart-hyperevm@0.1.0 so expired block-height samples
  stop permanently penalizing a formerly fast endpoint. Fresh lag remains
  penalized. The compiled dependency regression failed before and passed after.
- Added repeatable live measurements, isolated benchmark batching, bounded
  preflight retries, pacing, raw errors, source fingerprints, and strict gates.

REMAINING GAPS

PulseChain contract-read P50 was 171.30 ms against 111.85 ms direct (+59.45 ms).
Its per-method scores stopped learning from recovered PublicNode providers
following temporary slowdowns. The current router lacks bounded per-method
exploration or score expiry with revalidation; this recovery limitation remains.

OKC block-read P50 missed by 13.97 ms, and Moonbeam contract-read P50 missed by
11.78 ms. Those fastest healthy providers were sampled frequently; their gaps
do not establish the same recovery failure. Other median misses were generally
0.035-2.03 ms. They remain failures under the requested zero-tolerance rule;
these samples do not establish statistical equivalence or persistent regression.

EthereumPoW block-read P99 was 130.11 ms versus 108.02 ms direct. It has one
configured provider, so there is no second connection available for racing.
Pacing eliminated the provider rate limiting seen in the earlier unpaced run.

METHOD AND LIMITS

Read-only block-number and FENIX totalSupply requests; no transactions submitted.
Final validation compares production and direct transports in interleaved rounds.
All selected measurements match current policies, RPC code and patched router.
Historical runs are retained, but none replace a later failing final measurement.
The earlier baseline comparisons are separate experiments; they do not establish
same-time causal speedup percentages for the final patched source.

Quantiles use nearest rank over complete successful responses. Direct baselines
must finish every requested sample successfully. Failing or unavailable providers
are reported explicitly and cannot supply a fast-error latency baseline. Cold
requests and warm-up traffic are retained separately. Five consecutive measured
failures stop further attempts on that workload; partial series cannot pass.

These are single-host Bun network measurements. CORS checks are recorded, but
Bun does not enforce browser CORS. Browser/UI latency, chain-head canonicality,
and transaction submission performance are not certified. A 100-sample P99 is
preliminary tail evidence and does not guarantee future network latency.
